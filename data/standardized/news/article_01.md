# Thong bao mo cong dang ky ky tuc xa hoc ky Thu nam hoc moi

Ban Quan ly Ky tuc xa Dai hoc VinUni xin thong bao mo cong dang ky luu tru hoc ky Thu nam hoc moi:

## 1. Thoi gian dang ky
- Thoi gian bat dau: Tu 08:00 ngay 01/08 den 17:00 ngay 15/08 hang nam.
- Hinh thuc nop don: Truc tuyen thong qua Cong thong tin Sinh vien (Student Portal).

## 2. Doi tuong uu tien
- Uu tien so 1: 100% sinh vien nam nhat (Freshman) duoc dam bao cho o tai KTX.
- Uu tien so 2: Sinh vien nhan hoc bong toan phan hoac ho tro tai chinh dac biet.
- Uu tien so 3: Sinh vien co ho khau thuong tru ngoai tinh Ha Noi.

## 3. Muc phi phong va tien dat coc
- Phong tieu chuan 2 nguoi: 3.500.000 VND/sinh vien/thang (da bao gom wifi, nuoc sinh hoat va don phong chung hang tuan).
- Tien dien tinh theo chi so dong ho cong to rieng cua tung phong.
- Tien dat coc tai san: 2.000.000 VND (hoan tra khi ket thuc hop dong va ban giao phong day du).

Moi thac mac xin lien he van phong Ban Quan ly KTX qua email: studentaffairs@vinuni.edu.vn hoac hotline: 024-7108-9779.
