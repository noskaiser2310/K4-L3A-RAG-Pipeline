# Huong dan dang ky hoc phan va xu ly xung dot thoi khoa bieu

Phong Dao tao (Office of the Registrar) ban hanh huong dan chi tiet ve cong tac dang ky mon hoc:

## 1. Cac dot dang ky hoc phan
- Dot 1 (Pre-registration): Mo truoc hoc ky 4 tuan danh cho sinh vien dang ky theo ke hoach mau cua Vien chuyen nganh.
- Dot 2 (Add/Drop Period): Dien ra trong 2 tuan dau tien cua hoc ky moi. Sinh vien duoc phep them hoac huy mon hoc tu do ma khong bi ghi nhan diem W.

## 2. Quy dinh ve mon hoc tien quyet
- Sinh vien chi duoc dang ky hoc phan tiep theo khi da dat diem tu D tro len o hoc phan tien quyet (Prerequisite).
- Truong hop chua hoan thanh mon tien quyet nhung can hoc vu dac biet phai xin phe duyet cua Truong Khoa/Vien truong.

## 3. Gioi han tin chi toi da va toi thieu
- Toi thieu: 12 tin chi/hoc ky chinh.
- Toi da tieu chuan: 20 tin chi/hoc ky chinh.
- Toi da danh cho sinh vien co CGPA >= 3.6: 24 tin chi.

Moi yeu cau mo them lop hoac xin override si so sinh vien gui ticket qua he thong Helpdesk Dao tao.
