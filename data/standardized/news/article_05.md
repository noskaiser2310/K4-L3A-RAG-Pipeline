# Quy dinh quy doi diem ren luyen tu hoat dong ngoai khoa va cong tac xa hoi

Phong Cong tac Sinh vien ban hanh quy dinh danh gia diem ren luyen va quy doi gio cong dong:

## 1. Khung xep loai diem ren luyen
- Xuat sac: Tu 90 den 100 diem.
- Tot: Tu 80 den 89 diem (dieu kien bat buoc de duy tri hoc bong).
- Kha: Tu 65 den 79 diem.
- Trung binh: Tu 50 den 64 diem.
- Yeu/Kem: Duoi 50 diem (khong duoc xet khen thuong va hoc bong).

## 2. Tieu chi gio phuc vu cong dong (Community Service Hours)
- Moi sinh vien can tich luy toi thieu 20 gio hoat dong tinh nguyen/cong dong trong moi nam hoc.
- Cac hoat dong duoc tinh gio: Day hoc tinh nguyen, tham gia hien mau nhan dao, ho tro to chuc hoi nghi hoc thuat quoc te, tham gia chien dich mua he xanh.
- Minh chung gio phuc vu phai co chu ky xac nhan cua Truong ban to chuc hoac Co van cau lac bo.
